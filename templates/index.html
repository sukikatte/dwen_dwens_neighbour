Chinese Animal Guardians

Established in May 2015

Chinese Animal Guardians is a non-profit organization dedicated to protecting China's native wildlife.
We have consistently focused on the protection of endangered species and animal welfare,
raising public awareness of animal protection through education, advocacy, and practical action.

Our Journey

Endangered Species Protection Plan

2016-2018

A two-year protection program was launched for China's unique endangered animals (such as giant pandas and South China tigers). Through establishing wildlife reserves, carrying out habitat restoration work, and strengthening publicity of laws and regulations, the survival rate of these species has been effectively increased.

  • Established 5 national wildlife reserves
  • Restored habitat area exceeding 1000 square kilometers
  • Giant panda population increased by 15%

Animal Protection Educational Tour

2017-Present

Wildlife protection lectures are conducted nationwide, covering primary and secondary schools and communities, with over 200 lectures held and more than 50,000 direct participants. Through interactive activities and knowledge dissemination, public awareness and participation in animal protection have been enhanced.

Coverage

30 provinces and regions across China

Participants

Over 50,000 people

Stray Animal Rescue Initiative

2018-Present

Multiple stray animal rescue activities are organized annually, providing medical assistance and adoption services. To date, over 3,000 stray animals have been successfully rescued and helped to find new homes.

Ecological Environment Protection Project

2020-Present

Ecological environment protection projects are being carried out in multiple provinces, focusing on wetland and forest protection and restoration. Through afforestation and wetland restoration, ecosystem diversity has been increased, promoting the recovery of animal habitats.

Completed wetland restoration area: 500 hectares

Trees planted: 100,000

Biodiversity increase: 30%
